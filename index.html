What is GCAI?

GCAI stands for Genuinely Customizable Artificial Intelligence. Most AI assistants provide a fixed voice, a fixed appearance, and a fixed way of interacting. GCAI takes the opposite approach: you choose the colors and visual style, select the voice that responds, and set the wake word chime that activates it. It's built by Hoopslimbo, and the demo is free to try now.

FAQ

Is the Ondra Demo free?

Yes. The demo is completely free — no account, no payment, no install. Open it in your browser and go.

What do I need to run it?

A computer (Windows, Mac, or Linux) with a modern browser such as Google Chrome. The demo isn't available on phones or tablets.

What's the difference between the Ondra Demo and Ondra?

The Ondra Demo is the free demo: a customizable appearance, wake word, voices, themes, weather, recipes, and clock. Ondra is the full paid version of GCAI, with more on the way — including image generation, news briefings, and music.
