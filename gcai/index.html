The GCAI approach

Most AI assistants ship as a finished product with a short feature list. GCAI starts from the opposite assumption — that your assistant should do incredible things, and feel like yours while doing them.

Incredible features

Screen Coach and more — features that feel like science fiction, built to actually work.

Made truly yours

Appearance, voice, wake word — the details that make software feel personal are core features here, not afterthoughts.

Focused scope

Rather than one product that does everything adequately, GCAI pursues a small number of projects that each do one thing exceptionally well.

Projects

GCAI is a home for projects — each one a focused attempt to answer the same question: what does AI feel like when it can do incredible things and feels genuinely yours? A project starts as a working prototype, is refined through real use, and grows into a complete product only when it has earned its place.

There is one project so far. It is still early, and that is deliberate: we would rather get one thing right than ship five things half-finished.

In development

Ondra

GCAI's flagship project · Free demo available

Ondra is an AI assistant with incredible features on the way — Screen Coach and more — and it's yours: choose its appearance, its voice, and the wake word that calls it. The free Ondra Demo runs entirely in your browser: voice interaction, weather, and more, with the full version on the way.

Coming soon:

  • Screen Coach — Ondra watches your screen and talks you through any task, step by step.
  • World clock — ask the time anywhere in the world.
  • Computer access — ask what's on your computer and how much storage is left.
  • Connected accounts — Ondra gets things done across your accounts and services.
  • Notes — saveable, editable notes you can come back to anytime.
  • Open tabs — tell Ondra where to go and it opens the tab.
  • Email writer — generate an email from a sentence and send it.
  • Monthly rewards — a reward for every month you're subscribed.
  • Icon generator — design your AI's icon (background color, icon, icon color) for the browser tab.
