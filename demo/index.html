What you get in the demo

Everything below is live in the demo today.

Custom appearance

A visual identity that represents your assistant. Set its colors and style, and choose your wallpaper.

Wake word + chime

Say the wake word and Ondra answers with a chime you choose, so you know it's listening.

Voice interaction

Speak instead of typing. Choose from multiple voices for replies, or mute responses entirely.

Themes

Switch between themes to restyle the entire experience instantly.

Weather & recipes

Ask for the weather or look up a recipe — answers appear in a clean popup with live results.

Clock

A built-in clock, right where you need it.

One thing to know

The demo runs on computers only — Windows, Mac, or Linux with a modern browser such as Google Chrome. It isn't available on phones or tablets. Want the full thing? That's Ondra, coming soon.
